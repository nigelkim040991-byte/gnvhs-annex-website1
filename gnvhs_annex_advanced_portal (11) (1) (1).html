INSTITUTIONAL HERITAGE

School History & Heritage

Tracing the founding, community service, and technical-vocational leadership of Guiguinto National Vocational High School – Annex in Barangay Malis.

Year 2011

Establishment of GNVHS – Annex (Barangay Malis)

To decongest the main campus and eliminate burdensome daily commuting costs for families residing in Malis, Northville, and adjacent communities, the Department of Education (DepEd) Schools Division of Bulacan formally established the GNVHS Annex in Barangay Malis.

June 26, 2025 – Historical Landmark

Promotion of Kahryz D. Maninang (Sir Khaye) as First Principal I

Kahryz D. Maninang was officially elevated from Head Teacher VI to Principal I, becoming the very first official school principal of Guiguinto National Vocational High School – Annex, initiating a new era of digital transformation, modern vocational laboratories, and academic distinction.

DepEd Bulacan Vision, Mission & Core Values

The DepEd Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

The DepEd Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.

SCHOOL HEAD
Office of the School Principal Birthday: December 9

Kahryz D. Maninang (Sir Khaye)

Principal I • Guiguinto National Vocational High School – Annex

Formerly serving the campus as Head Teacher VI, Kahryz D. Maninang made history on June 26, 2025 when he was promoted to Principal I, becoming the very first official school principal of GNVHS Annex. Under his inspirational guidance, the campus in Barangay Malis has achieved rapid strides in technical-vocational facility upgrades, student empowerment, digital journalism excellence, and community engagement.

Junior High School (Grades 7–10)

Standard DepEd K-to-12 basic secondary curriculum integrated with exploratory TLE courses.

  • Core Disciplines: English, Math, Science, Filipino, AP, MAPEH
  • Hands-on Exploratory TLE in Drafting, Electronics, and Culinary Basics
  • Active Co-Curricular & Club Engagement (Math Club, English Guild, Journalism)

Senior High School (TVL Track)

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood track tailored for career competencies, entrepreneurship, or tertiary education.

  • ICT Strand: Computer Systems Servicing (CSS NC-II) & Web Prototyping
  • Home Economics: Bread and Pastry Production (BPP NC-II)
  • Work Immersion Partnerships with regional industries in Bulacan

Hands-On Technical Competency

TECHNICAL-VOCATIONAL EDUCATION (TVE)

Empowering students through applied mastery in Information & Communications Technology (ICT) and Home Economics (Bread and Pastry Production), establishing competencies aligned with TESDA industry standards and national certifications.

TVE - Information & Communications Technology (ICT)

CSS NC-II & WEB
TVE Name: Web & Digital Media Lab

Collaborative Interface & Tarpaulin Design

Students collaborating in the ICT Computer Lab, debugging code structures, formatting digital layout templates, and reviewing digital graphic assets under the guidance of TVE instructors.

TVE Name: Computer Systems Station

Applied Workstation Diagnostics & Digital Portals

Individual student workstations dedicated to user-interface prototyping, web application testing, and digital media production in a modular laboratory environment.

TVE - Home Economics (Culinary Arts, Bread & Pastry & Beauty Care Services)

BPP NC-II BEAUTY CARE NC-II
TVE Name: Confectionery Guild

Precision Cake Icing & Smoothing

Live stage demonstration showing spatula technique, turntable rotation, and uniform fondant/buttercream base finishing.

TVE Name: Commercial Baking Lab

Mise En Place & Ingredient Prep

Students executing ingredient scaling, egg grading, hygienic preparation, and multi-station pastry coordination in the food lab.

BEAUTY CARE NC-II Zoom
TVE Name: Hairdressing & Salon Services Station

Precision Hair Parting, Braiding & Salon Grooming

Hands-on student training in sanitary hair sectioning, client scalp ergonomics, intricate Dutch and French braiding techniques, and professional salon grooming hygiene in compliance with TVL Home Economics Hairdressing NC-II standards.

COSMETOLOGY NC-II Zoom
TVE Name: Cosmetology & Aesthetics Unit

Facial Make-up Artistry & Cosmetic Application

Applied practical assessment in daytime and corrective cosmetics, eyebrow defining, skin tone blending, hygienic brush sterilization, and client aesthetic consultation under DepEd TVL Beauty Care NC-II competencies.

TVE Name: Artisan Batter Workshop

Foam Whipping & Batter Folding

Hands-on practice in achieving stiff peaks, incorporating whipped egg foam, and folding batter to prevent deflation.
